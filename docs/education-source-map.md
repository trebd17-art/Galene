# SteadierMind education: source map for narration and video

Prepared October 12, 2026; reference mapping updated October 13, 2026. Covers the 15 written lessons in education-content.js. No videos have been generated as part of this work.

These are original educational summaries and examples, not quotations from the organizations below. References support related principles; they do not demonstrate that SteadierMind, its games, or its specific exercises produce clinical benefits. No organization listed here has endorsed the app.

## Lesson-by-lesson references

| Lesson | Principle to substantiate | Suggested references | Editorial limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understanding the alarm response | Anxiety can include anticipatory worry and physical responses. | [NIMH: Anxiety Disorders](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders); [NHS: Anxiety, fear and panic](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/anxiety-fear-panic/) | The alarm analogy is an educational metaphor. Do not equate anxiety with proof of safety or danger. Grounding is an optional coping activity, not a guaranteed treatment. |
| Making sense of physical sensations | Anxiety can have physical symptoms; symptoms can also have other causes. | [NHS: Anxiety, fear and panic](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/anxiety-fear-panic/); [NIMH: Anxiety Disorders](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders) | Preserve guidance to seek appropriate care for new or concerning symptoms. Do not diagnose the viewer. |
| Avoidance and getting support | Avoidance can maintain difficulties; professional support is appropriate when anxiety interferes with life. | [NHS: Anxiety, fear and panic](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/anxiety-fear-panic/); [NIMH: Anxiety Disorders](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/anxiety-disorders) | A manageable-step example is not a personalized exposure-treatment plan. Do not encourage entering unsafe situations. |
| Facts, predictions, and uncertainty | Examine evidence for thoughts rather than treating predictions as established facts. | [NHS Every Mind Matters: Reframing unhelpful thoughts](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/reframing-unhelpful-thoughts/) | Fact/prediction examples are teaching devices, not a diagnostic classification system. |
| Planning or a worry loop? | Distinguish practical problem-solving from repeated hypothetical worry. | [NHS Every Mind Matters: Tackling your worries](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/tackling-your-worries/) | "Worry loop" is plain-language shorthand. Do not suggest all extended thinking is unhelpful. |
| Making room for uncertainty | Practice responding to uncertainty without demanding complete reassurance. | [Centre for Clinical Interventions: Worry and Rumination](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Worry-and-Rumination), including its material on accepting uncertainty | Review the relevant module before narration. Do not imply that viewers should ignore real risks or that an affirmation treats compulsive checking. |
| Start smaller than you think | Set achievable challenges and practice manageable actions. | [NHS: Raising low self-esteem](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/tips-and-support/raise-low-self-esteem/) | Self-esteem and confidence overlap but are not identical. Avoid promising that practice will always remove anxiety. |
| A more supportive inner voice | Challenge overly negative beliefs and respond more kindly and realistically. | [NHS: Raising low self-esteem](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/tips-and-support/raise-low-self-esteem/); [NHS Every Mind Matters: Reframing unhelpful thoughts](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/reframing-unhelpful-thoughts/) | The friend-perspective prompt is an optional reflection exercise. It has not been separately validated as delivered in this app. |
| Recovering from a setback | Avoid interpreting one event as a global judgment; use manageable goals and adjustments. | [NHS: Raising low self-esteem](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/tips-and-support/raise-low-self-esteem/); [NIMH: Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health) | The missed-walk example is original. Sources provide general guidance, not direct evidence for that script. |
| Building a gentle wind-down | Sleep habits, schedule, environment, and substances can affect sleep. | [NHLBI: Healthy Sleep Habits](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/healthy-sleep-habits) | A wind-down may support rest; it does not guarantee sleep or treat all sleep disorders. |
| When thoughts follow you to bed | Worry-management strategies can include setting aside time or writing down concerns. | [NHS Every Mind Matters: Tackling your worries](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/tackling-your-worries/); [NHS Every Mind Matters: Sleep](https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-health-issues/sleep/) | Present a brief reminder as an optional adaptation, not as proven treatment for insomnia. |
| Rest without chasing perfect sleep | Persistent sleep difficulty deserves evaluation; CBT-I is an established treatment for chronic insomnia. | [NHLBI: Insomnia—Treatment](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/insomnia/treatment); [AASM clinical practice guideline, 2021](https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.8986) | The AASM guideline concerns chronic insomnia in adults. | Noticing your stress signals | Stress may affect physical symptoms, mood, thinking, and behavior. | [NHS: Stress](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/feelings-and-symptoms/stress/); [NIMH: Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health) | Symptoms are not specific to stress. Acknowledging practical and social stressors avoids placing all responsibility on individual habits. |
| Protecting a little space | Assertiveness includes clear, respectful communication and saying no. | [Centre for Clinical Interventions: Assertiveness](https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Assertiveness) | This is a stronger match than a generic stress article. The sample boundary is original; it is not advice for managing an unsafe relationship. |
| Rest and connection belong in the plan | Prioritize manageable goals, relaxing activities, and social connection. | [NIMH: Caring for Your Mental Health](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/caring-for-your-mental-health) | Tasks/rest/connection is an original organizing framework, not a validated prescription or fixed daily quota. |

## Peer-reviewed guideline for sleep content

American Academy of Sleep Medicine (2021). *Behavioral and psychological treatments for chronic insomnia disorder in adults: an American Academy of Sleep Medicine clinical practice guideline.* Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine. https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.8986

Use this guideline to substantiate the distinction between evidence-based insomnia treatment and general wellness wind-down activities. Do not use it to claim that SteadierMind provides CBT-I or is clinically proven.

## Recording and publishing references

For each script, retain a record of: lesson ID and version, relevant source passages/sections, current URL, date checked, reviewer name and qualifications, and agreed wording changes. A qualified mental health reviewer should assess the mental health scripts; persistent-sleep-difficulty content may warrant a reviewer with sleep expertise.

Suggested video end card:

> Sources and further reading: [organization and page title]. Full references are available in the lesson. SteadierMind provides general wellness education, not individualized care.

Use wording such as "informed by public health guidance." Avoid "clinically validated," "doctor approved," "proven to treat anxiety," or endorsement claims unless independently supported. Reuse original summaries; check permissions before copying source passages, graphics, or logos.
