Help & Support

Contact SteadierMind

For app help or privacy questions, email steadiermind@gmail.com. This inbox is for app support, not counseling or emergency help.

For a technical problem, describe what you were trying to do and your browser or device type. Do not include private journal entries, health details, or a full data export unless you deliberately choose to share them.

Where are my entries?

They are saved in this browser on this device. Another browser or device will not show the same entries. Clearing website storage or using a private browsing session can remove them.

Can I back up my data?

Open Settings and select Export My Data. This downloads a readable JSON file. Store it carefully; Settings can import the JSON backup after a validation and preview step. Printable HTML and CSV are reports, not restorable backups.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, select Clear Saved Data and confirm. Downloaded exports and copies you shared must be deleted separately.

What if saving does not work?

Check whether your browser allows website storage and has available space. If the app reports a save failure, keep the page open and retry before reloading.

Support beyond the app

For ongoing mental health concerns, consider a qualified healthcare professional. In the United States, call or text 988 for crisis support, or visit 988lifeline.org. For immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 911. Outside the United States, use local crisis and emergency services.
