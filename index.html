SteadierMind Games

Choose Your Game

Pick a short skill-building game. Each one trains calm, focus, confidence, or emotional balance.

🎮

Game Core Values

SteadierMind games are nonviolent, calming, and designed to teach mental wellness skills through interaction.

  • Manage anxiety instead of fighting it.
  • Practice small calm actions.
  • Build confidence through repetition.
  • Earn XP for healthy mental habits.

Centered

A strategy game about emotional balance, not emotional perfection.

🎮

Core Principle

You are not trying to eliminate anxiety. You are trying to maintain a healthy emotional ecosystem.

If the board becomes all green, that is not optimal. You need calm and focus working together while keeping anxiety from taking over.

🌈

Emotional Colors

Green = Calm Blue = Focus Red = Anxiety
⚖️

How You Win

Earn Harmony by maintaining balance across the board. Anxiety does not need to disappear for you to succeed.

  • Perfect Balance = +500 Harmony
  • Mostly Balanced = +250 Harmony
  • Unbalanced = +100 Harmony
  • Overwhelmed = 0 Harmony
⚡

Mental Energy & Powers

You begin with 100 Mental Energy. Every action costs energy, and energy slowly regenerates.

  • Deep Breathing = 5 Energy
  • Grounding = 10 Energy
  • Reframe = 12 Energy
  • Rest Day = restores Energy
