How Mind Points work

Daily check-in or mood entry: 5. New reflection: 10. First completion of a lesson: 5. Finished routine: 10. Game completion: 10. Completed breathing, grounding, or worry session: 5.

Editing an entry or repeating a completion tap earns no extra points. New-game levels reward their first completion. Rest in Sleep Wind-down has no points to chase. There are no mood-based rewards, penalties, rankings, or promised theme unlocks.

SteadierMind Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026. Bertrand Dennis III operates SteadierMind. This policy describes the initial free browser-based version.

What you choose to save

The app can store mood and calm-score check-ins, notes, saved reflections, setup preferences, your personalized plan, routine drafts, activity dates, Mind Points, and game and learning progress. Worry Release and Sleep Wind-down notes are saved only when you choose their save option.

Where your entries stay

Your saved app data is stored in this browser on this device using local storage. The current app does not send your entries to an app account or cloud database, and it does not sync them across devices. The Continue button does not create a server account.

Daily Reflection and Mood Tracker writing is saved when you choose Save or Update. Setup choices, routine drafts, and game progress can save automatically as you use them. Closing the app does not delete saved entries. Unsaved reflection and mood writing may be lost on reload.

Access, security, and retention

Data remains until you delete it, clear this website’s browser storage, or the browser removes it. Device or browser backup behavior depends on your settings. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view entries. SteadierMind does not provide password protection or app-level encryption for this local data. Avoid saving information you do not want accessible on a shared device.

Exporting your data

In Settings, choose Export My Data to download a JSON file containing the current saved-data snapshot, including saved entries, preferences, plans, drafts, and progress. The export is not uploaded by the app. It is a readable file that may contain sensitive information: store it carefully and share it only if you choose. This version does not offer an import or restore feature.

Deleting your data

In Settings, choose Clear Saved Data and confirm. This deletes SteadierMind’s stored entries, plans, setup preferences, and progress in this browser, then reloads the app. A non-personal setup flag may remain so the app can start fresh. It does not erase unrelated websites’ data.

Deletion does not remove downloaded exports, copies you shared, another browser or device’s data, or backups controlled by your device or browser. Delete those copies separately. Returning to the welcome screen does not delete your entries. Mood Tracker also lets you delete individual check-ins; other saved entries are included in Clear Saved Data.

Website requests and external links

The current application code contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell or transmit your saved entries to third parties. The intended website is steadiermind.com, with hosting planned through Netlify. Once deployed, delivering and securing the website may involve technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser information, and request times. The technical information handled and its retention depend on Netlify’s services and the configured hosting features. Browser-saved journal and mood entries are not included in the website’s normal page requests. See Netlify’s privacy policy for its provider practices.

When you open an educational source link, you visit a separate website with its own privacy policy. SteadierMind does not include your journal or mood content in those links. The app uses local storage for its features, rather than advertising cookies.
