Have you ever watched the clock and calculated how much sleep you could still get?
The harder you try to make sleep happen, the more frustrating being awake can feel.
Sleep can vary from night to night. A difficult night deserves care, without turning it into a judgment about how well you’re doing.
You can make room for sleep through your schedule, environment, and habits. But you can’t command an exact result.
If watching the time adds pressure, consider moving the clock out of easy view.
If you’re lying awake and becoming frustrated, you could get up for a quiet activity in dim light and return when you feel sleepy, if it’s safe and practical.
Persistent sleep problems deserve attention. They aren’t evidence that you haven’t tried hard enough.
A healthcare professional can consider possible causes and suitable treatment. For adults with chronic insomnia, cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia—called CBT-I—is an established treatment option.
SteadierMind’s wind-down is a wellness activity, rather than CBT-I.
Tonight, give yourself permission to support rest without grading your performance. And if difficulties continue, you can ask for help.
