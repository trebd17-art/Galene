When you make a plan for the day, what gets a place on the list?
Work. Errands. Messages. Things other people need.
Rest and connection can end up waiting until everything else is finished.
On a busy day, that moment may never arrive.
Consider making a little room for them in the plan you already have.
Alongside one necessary task, you could include a brief pause and a way to connect.
That might mean sitting quietly after an appointment, sending a message to someone you trust, or asking a friend for a short check-in.
Connection doesn’t have to mean a large gathering or a long conversation. Choose people and settings that feel supportive.
Rest can also take different forms. Think about what feels restorative to you, within the time and circumstances you have.
This is a flexible idea, rather than another checklist to complete perfectly.
If you’re isolated or carrying more than you can manage, support might come from a trusted person, a community resource, or a qualified professional.
Look at your next day. Where could a little rest or connection belong?
