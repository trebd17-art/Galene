Sometimes we wait to feel confident before we begin.
But confidence can develop through practice—even when the first attempt feels awkward.
The starting point doesn’t need to be impressive. It needs to be manageable.
‘Be more confident around people’ is a big goal. It doesn’t tell you what to do next.
‘Practice one question before a conversation’ gives you somewhere to start.
If that still feels too large, write the question down. Or say it privately first.
Think of something you’ve been putting off. What would a smaller version look like?
Opening the document instead of finishing the project. Practicing a greeting instead of planning a whole conversation. Asking a trusted person to help you prepare.
Choose something safe, specific, and within your control.
Afterward, notice what you attempted and what you learned. Feeling nervous doesn’t erase the effort.
You can repeat the step, adjust it, or ask for support.
Confidence doesn’t have to arrive all at once. Give yourself a chance to build experience at a pace you can work with.
