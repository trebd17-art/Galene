Have you ever said yes while already wondering how you would manage it?
It can feel uncomfortable to explain your limits, especially when you care about the person asking.
A boundary communicates what you can offer or what you need.
It might sound like: ‘I can’t take that on today.’
Or: ‘I can talk tomorrow, but I need to finish for tonight.’
You can be clear and respectful without giving a long explanation.
If it feels safe, start with a low-pressure situation. Perhaps protect a short break or clarify when you’re available to reply.
You might practice the sentence privately first.
The other person may still feel disappointed. A boundary can communicate your availability, but it cannot guarantee someone else’s reaction.
You also don’t need to offer an alternative unless you want to and can realistically follow through.
Some situations make boundaries difficult or unsafe. If speaking up could put you at risk, prioritize safety and seek trusted or professional support.
Think of one small piece of time or energy you’d like to protect.
What clear, fair sentence could help you express that?
